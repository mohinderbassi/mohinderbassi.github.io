Research Directions

DIRECTION 01

Proactive Information Triage in AI Systems

Transferring ITSM change-risk methodology to AI retrieval: ranking retrieved information not just by relevance, but by decision-criticality — accounting for recency, source authority, and contradiction signals. How do you surface the right information at the right moment at web scale?

IR Reranking LLM Eval RAG
DIRECTION 02

Resiliency Scoring for AI Retrieval Pipelines

Adapting multi-signal composite scoring from BMO's Resiliency Dashboard to measure retrieval pipeline health — combining freshness, source diversity, contradiction rates, and answer confidence. Designing dynamic routing under retrieval signal failure.

Pipeline Eval Anomaly Detection Systems ML

Rigorously measuring the retrieval quality gap between English and lower-resource languages (Hindi, Punjabi, Tamil, etc.) and proposing targeted interventions — informed by 8 years building and operating multilingual data systems for South Asian user populations.
